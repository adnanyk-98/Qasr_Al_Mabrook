# Qasr Al Mabrook - Brand & Reference Guidance

## 1. Brand Source

The supplied Qasr Al Mabrook brand PDF is the current brand-direction reference.

It should guide:

- Brand identity
- Logo treatment
- Color direction
- Visual tone
- Branded imagery treatment

Final logo and banner files will be provided separately.

## 2. Asset Rule

When final assets are supplied:

- Use the actual assets.
- Do not recreate the logo with text.
- Do not alter logo proportions.
- Preserve appropriate clear space.
- Use approved variants where supplied.

## 3. External Reference

The Mufaddal Fasteners website is a reference for:

- Catalogue information architecture
- Product discovery
- Product detail structure
- Enquiry CTA patterns
- General UX patterns

It is NOT a source for:

- Qasr Al Mabrook branding
- Product categories
- Business claims
- Product data
- Copy
- Visual identity

## 4. Design Decision

The final UI should be original and adapted to Qasr Al Mabrook rather than copied from a reference website.
