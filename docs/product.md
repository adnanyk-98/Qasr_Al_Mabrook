# Qasr Al Mabrook - Product Definition

## 1. Purpose

Qasr Al Mabrook is a multilingual digital product catalogue and enquiry platform for `qasralmabrook.com`.

The website is **not an e-commerce checkout platform in the initial release**. Its primary purpose is product discovery and conversion into enquiries.

## 2. Business Goal

Primary goal:

> Help customers discover the right product quickly and initiate an enquiry with minimal friction.

Primary conversion:

- Request Quote

Secondary conversions:

- WhatsApp Enquiry
- Call Sales
- Email Enquiry
- General Contact/Enquiry

## 3. Product Domain

The catalogue is intentionally generic.

Examples may include:

- Measuring tapes/tools
- Clothing and pyjamas
- Hair oil/personal care
- Household products
- Supermarket/general merchandise
- Future categories not yet known

The data model must therefore be configurable.

## 4. Initial Scope

### Public

- Home
- Product catalogue
- Category/subcategory pages
- Search
- Dynamic filters
- Product detail pages
- Product/variant enquiry actions
- Request Quote
- About Us
- Contact Us
- English and Arabic
- Responsive design
- SEO

### Admin

- Authentication
- Super Admin and Admin roles
- Product management
- Category/subcategory management
- Brand management
- Attribute management
- Variant management
- Specifications
- Image management
- Translation management
- Homepage/content configuration
- Quote/enquiry management
- Site settings

## 5. Explicitly Out of Scope Initially

- Product pricing
- Inventory/stock
- Online checkout
- Payments
- Customer accounts
- Order management
- Product documents/PDF datasheets
- Analytics
- Admin Arabic localization
- Native mobile app

## 6. Product Principles

1. Catalogue-first, not commerce-first.
2. Generic product model.
3. Configurable attributes and variants.
4. SEO-first.
5. Mobile-first and responsive.
6. English + Arabic from the beginning.
7. Admin-managed content.
8. Minimal unnecessary third-party infrastructure.
9. Tool-agnostic development documentation.
10. Repository documentation is the persistent project source of truth.
